Horizn – Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

What data we collect

None. Horizn collects no personal data whatsoever.

Local storage only

All countdown titles, dates, and settings are stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage API. This data never leaves your device.

No servers

Horizn has no backend server, no database, and no cloud sync. Nothing you type is transmitted anywhere.

No analytics or tracking

Horizn does not use Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or any other analytics or tracking service.

Permissions explained

storage — saves your countdowns locally.
tabs + scripting — displays the timer bar on web pages.
alarms — checks for expiry in the background.
notifications — alerts you when a countdown ends.
None of these are used to collect data.
